What our workshop can do

Sand casting

Brass, aluminium and kansa (bronze) cast in sand moulds — the traditional Moradabad way. Suited to urlis, bowls, bells, hardware and sculptural forms.

Sheet work & hand-hammering

Brass and copper sheet raised, spun and planished by hand. Hammer marks are laid deliberately — texture as a finish, not an accident.

Hand engraving & etching

Traditional nakkashi engraving and acid etching for florals, geometry or your own artwork, cut into the metal by working artisans.

Finishes

Antique gold, mirror polish, matte, oxidised, silver plate and powder coat — matched to your reference swatch before production begins.

Laser-cut ironwork

Wall art, screens and panels laser-cut from iron sheet to your vector files, then powder-coated or finished antique.

Wood & metal combinations

Sheesham wood turned and joined with brass or copper — lids, bases, handles and trays that pair warm timber with metal.

Custom sizes & fitments

Existing catalogue shapes rescaled, re-proportioned or re-fitted — different capacities, mounting hardware, stacking, food-contact liners.

Branding & packaging

Laser-etched logos, embossing, swing tags and printed retail-ready boxes, so the product lands shelf-ready under your brand.

From sketch to shipment

  1. Share your reference Send a drawing, photo, moodboard or physical sample with target sizes and quantities. We reply with feasibility notes and a quotation.
  2. Counter-sample, then physical sample We share counter-sample photos first for a quick steer, then make a physical sample — typically 7–14 days depending on complexity.
  3. Approval You approve the sample (or request revisions). Sizes, finish, weight and packaging are frozen as the production standard.
  4. Production with in-line QC The batch is made against the approved sample, with in-line checks on dimensions, finish and assembly as pieces come off the bench.
  5. Pre-dispatch sign-off Before anything ships, you receive photos and video of the finished, packed goods for sign-off.
