Process

How ordering works

  1. Send your RFQ

    Use the form below, email or WhatsApp. Tell us the products, quantities, finishes and destination — photos and reference links welcome.

  2. Quotation + Proforma Invoice

    We reply with a quotation in USD on the incoterm of your choice, then confirm the order request with a Proforma Invoice.

  3. Production

    Handmade in our workshop — typically 3–6 weeks, confirmed per order at quotation.

  4. QC + photo & video approval

    Every batch is inspected; you approve photos and video of your goods before anything is packed.

  5. Dispatch & documents

    Export packing, booking and the full document set — invoice, packing list and shipping papers — to your forwarder or ours.

No payment on this site — orders are confirmed by Proforma Invoice, and you pay by bank transfer only after you have it in hand.

Pricing, MOQ & payment

Wholesale pricing

Wholesale is quoted in USD — EXW Moradabad by default, with FOB Nhava Sheva / Mundra, CIF or DDP on request.

Retail prices shown elsewhere on this site are ₹ INR incl. GST, for India delivery only — they are not export prices.

MOQ & samples

  • Typical MOQ from 50 pcs per design — mixed-design orders welcome.
  • Samples: 1–5 pcs, quoted with courier to your door.

Payment

Export orders are paid by bank transfer (T/T) against Proforma Invoice. No payment is ever taken on this website.

Lead time

Handmade production typically runs 3–6 weeks; the exact schedule is confirmed on your quotation, per order.
