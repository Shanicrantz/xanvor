Buyer FAQ — importing from XANVOR

MOQ · Lead times · Payment · Shipping · Documents

Straight answers for importers, distributors and retailers working with a Moradabad workshop for the first time — or the fiftieth. If your question isn't here, ask us directly; we reply within one working day.

No payment on this site — orders are confirmed by Proforma Invoice.
How do I get a wholesale quote?

Send an order request through the RFQ form on our wholesale page, or write to hello@xanvor.com or WhatsApp +91 98377 60615 with the designs, quantities and destination port you have in mind. We reply within one working day with a written quotation under a reference number, so every follow-up stays traceable. No payment on this site — orders are confirmed by Proforma Invoice.

What are your MOQs?

MOQs start from 50 pieces per catalogue design and from 100 pieces for custom or modified designs. Samples are available in quantities of 1–5 pieces. If your trial order is smaller than our MOQ, tell us what you need — we will suggest the closest workable mix of designs.

How is wholesale pricing quoted?

Wholesale prices are quoted in USD as standard; EUR, GBP, AED or INR are issued on request — tell us your preferred quote currency on the RFQ, and the currency stated on your Proforma Invoice is the currency of settlement. We quote EXW Moradabad by default, and on request FOB Nhava Sheva or Mundra, CIF to your port, or DDP where serviceable. Each quotation states the Incoterm, validity period and packing assumptions, so there are no hidden extras later.

What are typical lead times?

Every piece is handmade, so production typically takes around 3–6 weeks after the Proforma Invoice is confirmed and the advance is received. The exact lead time is confirmed on your PI for each order, and we share progress updates from the workshop while your order is in production.

What payment terms do you accept?

We accept T/T (telegraphic bank transfer) against a Proforma Invoice — typically an advance to begin production, with the balance before dispatch, exactly as agreed on the PI. No payment on this site — orders are confirmed by Proforma Invoice, and our bank details appear only on that document.

Can I get samples first?

Yes — we encourage sampling before a bulk order. Samples (1–5 pieces) are charged at sample pricing plus courier, and the sample cost is adjusted against your subsequent bulk order. Sample lead times are shorter than production lead times and are confirmed with your quotation.

Do you do custom designs or private label?

Yes. We develop custom designs, finishes and sizes, and offer private-label options such as your logo and branded packing. Custom MOQs start from 100 pieces per design. See our OEM / ODM page for how development works, or send reference images with your order request.

How is quality checked?

Quality is checked in-line during production and again before dispatch. Before your order ships, we send pre-dispatch photos and video for your approval, so you see exactly what is leaving the workshop. Hand-finished pieces carry small natural variations; anything beyond that is flagged to you before dispatch, not after.

How are pieces packed for export?

Each piece is individually wrapped and packed with foam inserts inside rigid boxes, then consolidated into export-grade master cartons. Packing is specified on your quotation and Proforma Invoice, and we can adjust inner-pack quantities or add your barcodes and shipping marks on request.

What documents do you provide?

Every export shipment ships with a commercial invoice and packing list. Zenko Inc., the partnership firm behind XANVOR, is a GST-registered Indian exporter (GSTIN 09AAEFZ4419L1ZN). Other documents are arranged per order according to your import requirements — tell us what your customs broker needs when you place your order request.

Ready to request a quotation?

Send your designs, quantities and destination — we reply within one working day with a written quotation and reference number.
