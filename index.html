The House  ·  XANVOR  ·  Zenko Inc.

Metal handicrafts, hand-finished in Moradabad

XANVOR is the metal-handicrafts house brand of Zenko Inc., a partnership firm based in Moradabad — the city that has shaped brass for generations. We design, hand-finish and assemble brass, copper, silver-gilt and sheesham décor under one roof, and carry each piece outward through export.

Every XANVOR piece is hand-hammered and hand-finished by artisans in our Moradabad workshop. Small marks of the maker — a hammer line, a turn in the wood grain, a subtle shift in patina — are part of the character of a handmade object, not a defect.

The Craft  ·  What Our Workshop Can Do

Cast, raised and finished by hand

Send a sketch on a napkin or a full technical pack; either works. Our team turns references into counter-samples, counter-samples into approved specs, and approved specs into production batches.

I

Sand casting

Brass, aluminium and kansa (bronze) cast in sand moulds — the traditional Moradabad way. Suited to urlis, bowls, bells, hardware and sculptural forms.

II

Sheet work & hand-hammering

Brass and copper sheet raised, spun and planished by hand. Hammer marks are laid deliberately — texture as a finish, not an accident.

III

Hand engraving & etching

Traditional nakkashi engraving and acid etching for florals, geometry or your own artwork, cut into the metal by working artisans.

IV

Laser-cut ironwork

Wall art, screens and panels laser-cut from iron sheet to your vector files, then powder-coated or finished antique.

V

Wood & metal combinations

Sheesham wood turned and joined with brass or copper — lids, bases, handles and trays that pair warm timber with metal.

VI

Custom sizes & fitments

Existing catalogue shapes rescaled, re-proportioned or re-fitted — different capacities, mounting hardware, stacking, food-contact liners.

Stone is set by hand, so no two pieces are identical — mother of pearl laid by hand, natural agate and marble set into cast brass, copper raised and hammered on the bench.

Quality  ·  Nothing Ships Unseen

You approve it before it leaves

Hand-finished pieces carry small natural variations; anything beyond that is flagged to you before dispatch, not after.

In-line QC

Quality is checked in-line during production and again before dispatch, with checks on dimensions, finish and assembly as pieces come off the bench.

Photo & video approval

Every batch is inspected; you approve photos and video of your goods before anything is packed.

Export packing

Each piece is individually wrapped and packed with foam inserts inside rigid boxes, then consolidated into export-grade master cartons.

Documents

Every export shipment ships with a commercial invoice and packing list. Other documents are arranged per order, to your customs broker’s requirements.
